My Story

Built one weekend edit at a time

I started editing videos five years ago as a hobby — helping a friend cut his YouTube uploads on weekends, mostly just to see if I could make his footage feel less like raw clips and more like something worth watching.

Somewhere between the first jump cut and the hundredth late night render, I fell in love with storytelling itself — not just the edit, but the sound design and color grading that give a story its mood. What started as a favor turned into a full-time career.

“ I don't just cut clips together — I build a narrative that keeps the viewer watching until the very end.

That's the difference I try to bring to every project: structure first, polish second. My mission is simple — help creators and brands turn raw, unorganized footage into polished stories that actually grow their audience.
