How this Admin Panel works

A few things worth knowing before you start editing:

Every page on your site now pulls its text, images, services, awards, and portfolio projects from one shared content store instead of being hard-coded. When you save a change here, it's written to this browser's storage — and because every page reads from that same storage, your change appears the moment you open or refresh any page, no re-uploading needed.

The one thing to remember: this storage lives in this specific browser, on this specific device. It's perfect for building and previewing your site exactly the way it will look. But visitors browsing your site from their own phone or computer won't see admin edits made here unless you later connect this panel to a real server — see the message at the end of your project zip for the simple explanation.
